Care work is hard.
Reading the records shouldn't be.

We are exploring a small reading aid for foreign care workers in Japan: the kanji that show up in 介護記録, 申し送り, medication labels, and ケアプラン. There is no product yet. This page exists to find out whether it is worth building.

介護かいごの現場げんばで働はたらく外国人がいこくじんの方かたのために、記録きろくの漢字かんじを読よむ小ちいさな助たすけを作れないか考かんがえています。まだ製品せいひんはありません。本当に必要ひつようかどうかを、施設しせつ・登録支援機関とうろくしえんきかんの方かたに聞きいて確たしかめたいです。

For care facilities & 登録支援機関

We want to understand the work first.

Before building anything, we are looking for five short conversations with people who run or support care teams. There is no product to buy and no sales pitch.

ものを作る前まえに、現場げんばの話はなしを聞きかせてください。20〜30分ふんです。まだ製品せいひんはなく、売うり込こみはしません。

We will ask how your staff read 申し送り, 介護記録, medication labels, and ケアプラン; where kanji causes delays or repeated checks; and what would make a small pilot useful.

外国人がいこくじん職員しょくいんが記録きろくを読よむ流ながれ、手書てがきの申もうし送おくり、薬くすりのラベル、ケアプランについて伺うかがいます。

  • 20–30 minutes, free, by video call or phone.
  • No patient or resident information needed.
  • If it fits, 1–2 organizations may be invited to a free 4-week pilot later. Nothing is decided in advance.
  • You can stop the conversation at any time.

We are not affiliated with any government body, exam organization, or facility. This is an independent concept.

Straight answers

Questions we should answer honestly.

よくある質問しつもんにお答こたえします。

Is this available now?

No. This is a concept page. There is no app, no account, and no content library yet. The waitlist exists to measure whether the idea is worth building.

いいえ。まだ製品せいひんはありません。このページは、作つくる価値かちがあるかを確認かくにんするためのものです。

Is it free? How much will it cost?

Pricing is not decided. We are not collecting payment, and there is no paid plan to buy. The 14-day validation is designed to cost 0 yen in cash; that does not tell us what a future product should cost.

価格かかくはまだ決きめていません。お金かねは受うけ取とりません。

Are you an official service?

No. This is an independent concept. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the Immigration Services Agency, SSSC (社会福祉振興・試験センター), any care facility, or any 登録支援機関.

公式こうしきサービスではありません。政府せいふ・試験しけん機関きかん・施設しせつと関係かんけいありません。

Will it help me pass the 介護福祉士 exam?

We cannot promise that. This is not a past-exam question bank, and no exam result is guaranteed. The concept focuses on reading kanji in daily care records. If it helps with exam study, that would be a bonus, not a claim we can make yet.

合格ごうかくは保証ほしょうできません。これは過去問かこもんの問題集もんだいしゅうではありません。

Is this medical or care advice?

No. This is language-learning content about words, not medical, nursing, or care instructions. If you are unsure about a resident’s condition or a procedure, follow your facility’s rules and ask a qualified senior staff member. For an emergency, follow your facility’s emergency procedure and contact local emergency services.

これは言葉ことばの学習がくしゅうです。医療いりょうや介護かいごの指示しじではありません。わからないときは、施設しせつの手順てじゅんに従したがってください。

What happens to my data?

The email waitlist uses your email address, your role, your first language, and an optional message. Please do not include patient or resident information. Your details are used only to contact you about this concept. Reply to any email to ask us to delete them.

メールアドレス、立場たちば、言語げんごだけを聞ききます。患者かんじゃさんの情報じょうほうは書かかないでください。

Why only English and Japanese?

Many care workers in Japan speak Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Filipino, or Nepali. We know English and Japanese alone are not enough. This first page uses English and easy Japanese for validation; other languages would need native reviewers and care-specific checks before launch.

ベトナム語ご・インドネシア語ご・マレー語ご・フィリピン語ご・ネパール語ごを使つかう方かたもいます。このページはまず英語えいごとやさしい日本語にほんごだけです。

Do you have testimonials or user numbers?

No. There is no product, so there are no users and no testimonials. We will not invent them. If real quotes are added later, they will be attributed, used with permission, and shown without edited claims.

製品せいひんがないので、ユーザーも声こえもありません。うその声こえは作つくりません。

When will it launch?

No date is set. Development starts only if the validation thresholds are met: 25 valid worker waitlist signups, 3 B2B inquiries with at least one written pilot intent, 5 completed interviews with the right pain signal, and a committed care/Japanese reviewer. If those are not met, we will not pretend the product is coming.

できる日ひはまだ決きまっていません。必要ひつような条件じょうけんを満みたさないときは、作つくらない決定けっていをすることもあります。
