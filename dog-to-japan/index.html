The rule sequence Source rules
APHIS + MAFF AQS
  1. 01

    Microchip + rabies vaccine 1

    ISO 11784/11785 microchip on the day of or before vaccine 1. The dog must be at least 91 days old, with birth date counted as day 0.

  2. 02

    Rabies vaccine 2

    More than 30 days after vaccine 1, with vaccine 1 counted as day 0. It must also fall within the 1st vaccine's duration of immunity.

  3. 03

    FAVN titer blood draw

    On the day of vaccine 2 or later. The result must be ≥ 0.5 IU/ml at a lab approved by Japan's MAFF.

  4. 04

    The 180-day wait

    The blood draw is day 0. Arrival must be at least 180 days later. If it is earlier, the dog stays at an AQS facility for the remaining days.

  5. 05

    AQS notice + arrival

    Notify AQS at least 40 days before arrival. Arrival must be within the vaccine's effective period and the titer's 2-year validity.

For a compliant arrival, APHIS notes quarantine of less than 12 hours. This tool checks the dates, not the whole file.

Why the dates matter

A date error becomes quarantine time.

If the blood draw is too early or arrival lands too soon, the rule has a consequence: the dog is quarantined at an AQS facility for the remaining days of the 180-day wait.

A compliant arrival is different — APHIS notes quarantine of less than 12 hours. The gap is what this planner is designed to catch before you book anything.

You have chosen not to hand the whole move to a shipper. That does not mean guessing at a date sequence that runs from a microchip through two vaccines, a titer, a 180-day wait, a titer expiry, and an AQS notice.
