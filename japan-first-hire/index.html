The first-hire sequence

Simplified
  1. Before hiring Written working conditions — required under the Labor Standards Act, Article 15.
  2. Day 1–5 Health & pension enrollment — social insurance procedures within 5 days of hire.
  3. Within 10 days Labor insurance establishment notice to the Labor Standards Inspection Office, and employment insurance establishment notice to Hello Work.
  4. Within 50 days Estimated premium filing (概算保険料申告).
  5. By the 10th
    of next month     Employment insurance qualification notice (資格取得届).
  6. Within 1 month Tax office notice of a salary-paying office.
  7. Before overtime 36 Agreement — required before any overtime work.
  8. At 10+ regular
    workers     Rules of Employment — required for workplaces with 10 or more regular workers.

A simplified view, not a complete legal checklist. Deadlines and requirements can change. Verify with the public sources and a licensed 社労士.

Honest boundaries

What this is not.

These boundaries are not fine print. They decide what we will build, what we will decline, and when we will tell you to call a licensed professional instead.

  • Not a 社労士 filing service. We do not prepare, review, or submit labor or social insurance filings for clients.
  • Not legal, tax, or immigration advice. Labor and social insurance questions: a licensed 社労士. Tax: a 税理士. Immigration: an 行政書士 or 弁護士.
  • Not an official product. No affiliation with JETRO, MHLW, Hello Work, or any government body.
  • Not a replacement for professional review. Templates are general information and must be adapted for your situation.
  • Not an Employer of Record (EOR) or payroll provider. We can explain the questions to ask, not employ your staff or run payroll.
  • Not a shortcut to Japan market entry. The wedge is the first hire, not incorporation, tax, or ongoing HR.
